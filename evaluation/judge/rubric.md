# Judge rubric (fixed before grading)

Grade only the CONTINUATION (the text after the given opening), judged as the ending of the
opening's story. Based on the TinyStories paper's criteria, plus plot. Each score 1-10.

| Criterion | 1-3 | 4-6 | 7-8 | 9-10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Grammar**: spelling, agreement, punctuation, complete sentences | Frequent errors; hard to read | Several errors, meaning still clear | Minor slips | Essentially error-free |
| **Consistency**: fits the opening; characters, names, facts and the mid-sentence join stay coherent | Ignores or contradicts the opening | Partly fits; some contradictions or confused characters | Fits, with small slips | Fully coherent with the opening |
| **Plot**: events make sense in order and reach a resolution | No sensible sequence | Some sequence, but illogical steps or no ending | Sensible, minor gaps | Clear, sensible arc with an ending |
| **Creativity**: interesting, non-generic ideas or details (for a children's story) | Repetitive or empty | Generic, predictable | Some original touches | Distinctive and imaginative |

Rules:
- Judge the text, not the length; a story cut off by the token limit is not penalized for
  stopping mid-sentence, but text that drifts out of the story (e.g. into unrelated
  dialogue formats or Q&A) counts against Consistency and Plot.
- Give each score independently, then one sentence of justification.
- The model identity is hidden; do not try to infer it.
